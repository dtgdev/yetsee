# Unified Investigation Workspace

Alpha 0.5 makes the Investigation the primary user-facing object in YetSee.

Canonical workspace route:

`/investigations/{id}?lens=<overview|evidence|structure|reasoning|history|compare>`

Scientific lenses answer one question each:

- Overview — What is happening?
- Evidence — What supports or contradicts this?
- Structure — How is everything connected?
- Reasoning — What does the evidence imply?
- History — How has understanding evolved?
- Compare — Where do models agree or disagree?

Forecast and Simulation are reserved as future lenses rather than standalone top-level pages.

Standalone system pages may still exist for administration, registries, discovery intake, and global operations. Normal scientific work belongs inside an investigation.
